1. What this policy covers

This Cookie Policy explains the cookies and similar browser technologies used by the current Athion Studio website, operated by Athion Private Limited.

2. Cookies

The current Athion Studio website does not intentionally use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, retargeting cookies or other tracking cookies to profile visitors.

We therefore do not use a cookie banner for advertising or analytics consent on the current version of the website.

3. Temporary browser storage

The website may use temporary browser session storage for limited functionality. For example, the site can use session storage to remember that its introductory visual sequence has already been shown during the current browser session.

This is not used to identify you, build a personal profile, advertise to you or analyse your behaviour.

4. Contact form and third-party processing

When you submit the website contact form, the information you provide is transmitted to service providers used to operate the form and website workflow. This processing is necessary to receive and manage your enquiry. The contact-form process is described in more detail in our Privacy Policy.

5. Browser controls

You can generally control cookies and browser storage through your browser settings. Disabling browser storage may affect limited website functionality, including the introductory experience, but should not prevent you from viewing the core website.

6. Changes to this Cookie Policy

If Athion introduces analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or other technologies that materially change the way this website stores or accesses information, this Cookie Policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy, contact hello@athionstudio.com.
